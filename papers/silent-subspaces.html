The dimension of silent subspaces in finite-sampling truncations of the Weil quadratic form

Mehmet Akif Oltulu · Maovera AI Research (independent), Istanbul, Türkiye
2026-09-12 · version v2 · doi:10.5281/zenodo.22730006

Full text (PDF) · Zenodo record

Abstract

We identify and determine the dimension of the silent subspace of a finite-sampling truncation: the space of test vectors whose in-band contribution to the truncated Weil quadratic form vanishes identically. Our main result is exact and unconditional: if L collects N Fourier-sampling conditions together with one pole condition on a d-dimensional space of even functions, then dim ker L = d − rank L ≥ max(0, d − N − 1), with equality in the lower bound precisely when the conditions are independent. Two independent prolate computations, carried out at 65-digit working precision and at two different Legendre cutoffs, confirm a two-dimensional joint kernel in both test cells, with agreement to about 22 relative digits. We further give a min–max eigenvalue bound over the silent subspace and, under positivity assumptions, a quantitative bound ‖PH⊥ v‖2 ≤ λ/b on the out-of-kernel component of an eigenvector, together with an explicit 2 × 2 example showing that this bound cannot be strengthened to exact containment. The dimension theorem explains, from first principles, the empirical design rule d = N + 3 used in earlier truncation experiments.

Keywords: Weil quadratic form, finite sampling, silent subspace, prolate functions
