Formal verification of the index law and the resolvent lock in Lean 4

Abstract

Theorem 5(a) (index law, even sector) and Theorem 6 (resolvent lock) of [1] are formally verified in the Lean 4 proof assistant [2] with the Mathlib library [3]. For a real symmetric matrix Q of Connes–van Suijlekom type on I = {−N, …, N}, Theorem 5(a) states Nc(ξ) + d(ξ) = κ(λ) for a simple eigenvalue λ with even eigenvector ξ ∉ ℝe0, and Theorem 6 states Nc(x) + d(x) + [x0 < 0] = κ(µ) for the resolvent vector x = (Q − µ)−1 e0: the zeros of a single polynomial count the eigenvalues below a level. Both theorems carry complete formal proofs; the axiom audit lists exactly the three standard axioms of Lean’s foundations. The formal proofs follow the route of [1]—commutator identity, rank-one operator Dη′, Q-symmetry, norm identity and Pontryagin balance—and make three finite-dimensional steps explicit: the Sylvester bridge between eigenvalue counts and the negative index, the root count of a separable real polynomial, and the Pontryagin balance for a form whose kernel is spanned by one eigenvector. The library passes a negative control of 60 deliberately corrupted statements, continuous integration on an independent machine, and numerical cross-checks on 398 and 1210 random instances. The verified state is tag v1.0, commit 08a1c67, of the public repository [4].

Scope. Formally verified (Lean 4 / Mathlib): Theorems 5(a) and 6 of [1], finite dimension. Measured: numerical cross-checks (Table 4). Continuation: Theorem 5(b) (odd sector) and the operator-level theorems of [1].
