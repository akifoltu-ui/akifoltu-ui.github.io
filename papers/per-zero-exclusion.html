Per-zero exclusion bounds free of assumptions on the zeros above the band, sharpened by a formally verified zero-free half-plane

Mehmet Akif Oltulu · Maovera AI Research (independent), Istanbul, Türkiye
2026-10-08 · version 0.4.1 · doi:10.5281/zenodo.23237558

Full text (PDF) · Zenodo record

Abstract

A positive value λ of the Weil quadratic form, truncated to the window [−h, h], h = ½ log 13, evaluated at its pole-conditioned minimiser and computed on the geometric side independently of the zeros, is converted into a bound |Re ρk − ½| ≤ δ∗k for individual zeros ρk of ζ(s), with no assumption on the location of the infinitely many zeros above the band; the other in-band zeros enter at their on-line ordinates, a range verified rigorously by Platt and Trudgian. The price of this independence is an envelope of the explicit formula taken over the whole width of the region in which zeros may lie. We feed into this envelope the zero-free half-plane Re s > 7/8 of OpenAI (2026), whose Lean formalisation we compiled independently from a fresh clone and audited (7 061 modules; axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound only; negative control detected). The admissible width drops from 1/2 to 3/8, the envelope factor from 13.20 to 6.04, and every bound tightens by the same factor 1.501. In the cell q = 13, Ω = 75.55, d = 21 we obtain |Re ρ1 − ½| ≤ 3.50×10−19, |Re ρ5 − ½| ≤ 9.93×10−15, |Re ρ10 − ½| ≤ 4.31×10−10, |Re ρ18 − ½| ≤ 5.74×10−3. The envelope is built on a two-dimensional grid across the whole admissible strip, its Abel summation includes the boundary term, and condition (C2) is computed at 120 digits; the one remaining input, the contribution (A) of the zeros above G2 = 2000, is quantified by doubling G2 at equal grid spacing: it changes the envelope by 6.4 × 10−4 and the first bound by 3.4 × 10−4 relative. The cost of independence from the zeros above the band, measured as the ratio to the conditional bound, falls from 4.36 to 2.90. To our knowledge this is the first use of a formally verified zero-free region as an input to a finite positivity certificate.

Keywords: Riemann zeta function, Weil explicit formula, zero-free region, per-zero exclusion, positivity certificate, Lean formal verification
