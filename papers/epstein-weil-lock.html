A Weil-positivity lock detects and counts genuine off-line zeros of an Epstein zeta function

Mehmet Akif Oltulu · Maovera AI Research (independent), Istanbul, Türkiye
2026-10-18 · version 1.0.1 · doi:10.5281/zenodo.23459816

Full text (PDF) · Zenodo record

Abstract

Weil’s explicit formula turns the location of the zeros of a zeta function into the sign of a quadratic form, and the resolvent lock of [3] reads that sign, together with the exact count of negative directions, from the real zeros of a single polynomial. We build the truncated Weil form of a two-dimensional Epstein zeta function directly from its own data—the gamma factor, one constant and a finite set of generalised primes obtained from the logarithmic derivative of its Dirichlet series by an exact recursion—so that the construction uses the function’s own data only. The Epstein family has no Euler product and, for suitable lattices, genuine zeros off the critical line [5]. On the lattice D = 7, whose zeros all lie on the line, the lock is closed (κ = 0). On the lattice ∆ = 0.800942… the lock opens with κ = 4, and the index law splits the count as 4 = 2 + 2 + 0. Subtracting the zero-side contributions of the three off-line quadruples inside the band closes the lock again (κ = 0); subtracting any single one leaves it open. Along the way the explicit-formula identity, evaluated from function values, located two off-line zeros beyond the published one, at heights 17.21 and 23.06 (the latter with real part 1.326). All numbers were reproduced on two machines.

Scope. Computed (100 digits, two machines, identical to the printed digits): the Epstein Weil form, its eigenvalue counts and the lock readings of Table 3. Computer-verified (interval arithmetic): the lock’s zero-count certificates. Verified against independent methods: the construction reproduces the calibrated ζ machine of [3] to 1.5 · 10−58 and the explicit-formula identity to 3.9 · 10−32.

Keywords: Weil explicit formula, Weil positivity, Epstein zeta function, off-critical zeros, generalised primes, Pontryagin index, resolvent lock, Chowla–Selberg formula
